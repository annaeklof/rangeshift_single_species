Dear Prof. Franklin,\\

My co-authors and I are pleased to submit our manuscript \mytitle for publication in \myjournal. 

In this manuscript we develop a novel theoretical framework for modeling species range shifts. We focus on three factors recognized for their importance for range shifts; dispersal distance for the species, configuration for suitable habitat in the regional landscape, and speed of climate. To justify the ecological relevance of our model we use empirical data on species' dispersal distances and current and future landscape configuration for a set of European mammals. We show that in particular the specific configuration of suitable habitat in the landscape have far reaching consequences. The results have implications for biodiversity scenarios and conservations planning. 

Because of this, we hope that you will agree that our manuscript merits publication in \myjournal. We look forward to your response.
